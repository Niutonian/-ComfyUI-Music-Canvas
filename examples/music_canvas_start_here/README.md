# music_canvas_start_here

Open [music_canvas_start_here.json](music_canvas_start_here.json) in ComfyUI and load your own song. Queue once to render the first 12 seconds as two 6-second clips with visual continuity at 0.25 MP and eight steps. Lyrics transcription is off. Follow the [installation guide](../../README.md) for the models and Ollama setup.
